About me

I am a second-year Computer Science student with a background as an Electronics Technician. Unlike many peers who focus on high-level web development, I am passionate about what happens 'under the hood.' My focus is on C, C++, and Operating Systems because they offer total control over hardware and performance.

I approach programming with an analytical mindset—I break down complex errors into smaller, solvable problems. Currently, I am deepening my knowledge of Linux environments and network protocols. My career goal is to combine low-level programming with cybersecurity to build high-performance systems that are resilient to exploits and crashes.
